# Primary Maths national curriculum audit

Source: *PRIMARY national curriculum – Mathematics* (Department for Education, September 2013, reference DFE-00180-2013), supplied as `PRIMARY_national_curriculum_-_Mathematics_220714.pdf`.

This audit covers the statutory requirements only. The document's notes and guidance are useful teaching advice but are explicitly non-statutory, so they are not counted as required attainment content. Parent bullets and their indented statutory sub-bullets are counted separately.

## Year 6 corrections made by this audit

- Week 22 Day 4 now explicitly teaches finding pairs of values that satisfy an equation with two unknowns.
- Week 22 Day 5 now explicitly teaches systematic enumeration of combinations of two variables.
- Week 24 Day 1 now explicitly compares shapes with equal area and different perimeter.
- Week 26 Day 5 now explicitly teaches drawing 2-D shapes from given dimensions and angles and recognising/building 3-D shapes from nets.

Each correction is represented in the curriculum map, lesson record, editable teacher plan, pre-teach PDF, three differentiated one-page pupil worksheets with answer pages, and a click-paced teaching PowerPoint. The changed summer assessment also samples the corrected content.

The machine-readable evidence report is `public/curriculum-plans/maths/national-curriculum-audit.json`. Run `python scripts/audit_primary_national_curriculum.py` to recreate it and fail if a year loses a lesson, week, term evaluation, or required curriculum evidence group.
